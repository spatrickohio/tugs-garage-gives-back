Tug's Garage Gives Back

Using the skills we have, bringing good people together, and helping someone move forward.

A community project from Tug's Garage in Shelby, Ohio.

Our Mission

Tug's Garage Gives Back exists to help people in our community move forward by using the skills, experience, resources, and relationships of Tug's Garage to create practical opportunities that can make a real difference.

We plan to start small by supporting young people beginning careers in the skilled trades, including graduating vocational students who may need quality tools or equipment to help them get started.

As the program grows, our long-term vision includes larger projects, including providing safe, reliable transportation to individuals and families facing transportation hardship.

We believe this can become bigger than one garage. By bringing together local businesses, skilled tradespeople, automotive professionals, sponsors, viewers, schools, and members of our community, we hope to turn the skills and resources people already have into opportunities that help someone take their next step.

Where We're Starting

Starting Small

🧰 Supporting the Skilled Trades

Our first goal is simple: help a young person who has chosen to build a future with their hands.

Tug's Garage Gives Back plans to work with local vocational education programs and community partners to identify graduating students entering skilled trades such as automotive technology, diesel and agricultural equipment, electrical, welding, construction, and other hands-on careers.

Depending on the student's chosen trade and individual needs, assistance could include a starter toolbox, hand tools, specialty tools, safety equipment, or other essential equipment that helps that young person begin their career.

The idea isn't to hand someone everything they will ever need. It's to help a hardworking young person get a solid start.

Future Vision

🚗 Reliable Transportation

As Tug's Garage Gives Back grows and develops stronger community partnerships, our vision is to take on larger projects involving dependable transportation.

The goal would be to acquire an affordable used vehicle and use the skills, experience, and resources of Tug's Garage to thoroughly inspect, repair, service, and prepare that vehicle for an individual or family who genuinely needs reliable transportation.

Completed vehicle projects would be independently inspected by a qualified automotive professional who was not responsible for the repairs before the vehicle is given to its new owner.

How Local Businesses Can Help

Helping doesn't always mean writing a check. Local businesses, tradespeople, automotive professionals, and community members may already have something that can help another person get started.

Tools & Equipment Hand tools, tool storage, trade-specific equipment, safety gear, and other items that can help a young person begin a skilled-trade career.
Professional Services Vehicle inspections, alignments, diagnostics, towing, detailing, and other professional services for future projects.
Parts & Supplies Automotive parts, tires, fluids, shop supplies, repair materials, and other items needed to complete future projects.
Labor & Expertise Skilled professionals willing to contribute their time, knowledge, mentorship, or specialized abilities.
Community Partnerships Schools, vocational programs, local organizations, and businesses willing to help identify needs and connect good people.
Vehicles Suitable vehicles that may someday become candidates for a Tug's Garage Gives Back transportation project.
Sponsorship & Financial Support As the program develops, financial support may help cover tools, parts, professional services, and other project expenses.

Our Promise

We want the help to be practical. Whether we're putting tools into the hands of a young tradesperson or someday handing someone the keys to a dependable vehicle, the goal is to provide something useful that helps that person move forward.

Safety and reliability will come first on every vehicle project. A vehicle isn't finished simply because it looks good or makes good content. Our goal is for completed vehicle projects to receive an independent inspection from a qualified automotive professional who was not responsible for the repairs.

People come first. No one should have to put their hardship on display in exchange for help. Anyone receiving assistance through Tug's Garage Gives Back will be treated with dignity and respect. Personal stories will only be shared with the person's knowledge, participation, and permission.

We intend to document projects honestly so our community can see where the help came from, what was accomplished, and what ordinary people and local businesses can do when they work together.

Get Involved

Whether you have tools, equipment, professional services, expertise, parts, or simply an idea for how you might help, we'd like to hear from you.
