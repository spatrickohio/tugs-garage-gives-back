Tug's Garage Gives Back

Using the skills we have, bringing good people together, and helping someone move forward.

A community project from Tug's Garage in Shelby, Ohio.

Our Mission

Tug's Garage Gives Back exists to help individuals and families in our community who are facing transportation hardship by providing safe, reliable transportation to those who genuinely need it.

Our mission is to acquire an affordable used vehicle and use the skills, experience, and resources of Tug's Garage to thoroughly inspect, repair, service, and prepare that vehicle for someone who needs dependable transportation.

We believe this can be bigger than one garage. By bringing together local businesses, automotive professionals, sponsors, viewers, and members of our community, we hope to turn the skills and resources people already have into something that can make a real difference.

What We're Building

🚗 Reliable Transportation

Our long-term goal is to find affordable used vehicles, repair and service them properly, have the finished vehicles independently inspected by qualified automotive professionals, and place them with individuals or families who genuinely need dependable transportation.

🧰 Supporting the Skilled Trades

Giving back doesn't have to begin with a car. Tug's Garage Gives Back also hopes to support young people beginning careers in the skilled trades. That may include helping a graduating vocational student get started with quality tools and equipment needed to begin his or her career.

How Local Businesses Can Help

Helping doesn't always mean writing a check. Businesses, professionals, and community members may have skills, services, equipment, or resources that can help make a project possible.

Professional Services Vehicle inspections, alignments, diagnostics, towing, detailing, and other automotive services.
Parts & Supplies Repair parts, tires, fluids, tools, shop supplies, and other materials needed to complete a project.
Labor & Expertise Skilled professionals willing to contribute their time, experience, or specialized abilities.
Vehicles Suitable vehicles that may someday become candidates for a Tug's Garage Gives Back project.
Community Partnerships Local organizations and businesses interested in helping identify needs and bringing people together.
Financial Support Future sponsorship or other support may help cover parts, services, and project expenses as the program develops.

Our Promise

Safety and reliability come first. A vehicle isn't finished simply because it looks good or makes good content. Our goal is for completed vehicle projects to receive an independent inspection from a qualified automotive professional who was not responsible for the repairs.

People come first, too. No one should have to put their hardship on display in exchange for help. Anyone receiving assistance through Tug's Garage Gives Back will be treated with dignity and respect. Personal stories will only be shared with the person's knowledge, participation, and permission.

We intend to document these projects honestly — the problems we discover, the repairs, the people and businesses who help, and the final result — so our community can see what ordinary people can accomplish when they work together.

Get Involved

Tug's Garage Gives Back is still being built from the ground up. We're interested in hearing from local businesses, automotive professionals, community organizations, and others who believe in what we're trying to accomplish.
